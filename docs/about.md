# About Monk Stories

**Monk Stories** was created to bridge timeless philosophical wisdom with modern competitive realities.

In modern workplaces and high-stakes organizations, technical skill is only half the battle. The other half is navigating **human nature**: envy, hidden agendas, power dynamics, shifting loyalties, and passive-aggressive politics.

Our stories draw from:
- **Indian Epics & Puranas**: The strategic counsel of Vidura (*Vidura Neeti*), the tactical games of Shakuni, and Krishna's teachings in the *Mahabharata*.
- **Vedic & Classical Wisdom**: Chanakya's ruthless pragmatism (*Chanakya Neeti* & *Arthashastra*) and Panchatantra's fables on deceit and alliance.
- **Telugu Satakam Literature**: The penetrating, direct moral philosophy of Yogi Vemana (*Vemana Satakam*) and Baddena (*Sumati Satakam*).
- **Modern Power Dynamics**: Robert Greene's *The 48 Laws of Power*, Machiavelli, and contemporary emotional intelligence research.

Each day brings a narrative parable, a deep quote, an original brush-stroke artwork, and an actionable workplace takeaway.
